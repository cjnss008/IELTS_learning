研究团队把原子无线电接收技术扩展到视频接收，实验说明装置与激光参数如何影响响应、分辨率及数据速率；文中展现研究原型与潜在用途，不是已普及的量产产品。

- **研究成果**：把原子无线电接收器改造成可检测并显示彩色视频/游戏；报道声称首次展示视频接收。
  证据 P1 UTF16 [0,178)：Researchers at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) have adapted their atom-based radio receiver to detect and display live color television and video games.
- **实用兴趣限定**：可能体积更小、更耐噪，可能帮助远程/应急无线电；could 不等于已经商业验证。
  证据 P2 UTF16 [180,351)：Atom-based communications systems are of practical interest because they could be physically smaller and more tolerant of noisy environments than conventional electronics.
- **高能态**：Rydberg原子对包括无线电信号的电磁场异常敏感，并可做关联SI的功率测量。
  证据 P3 UTF16 [464,614)：NIST’s receiver uses atoms prepared in high-energy “Rydberg” states, which are unusually sensitive to electromagnetic fields, including radio signals.
- **装置版本区别**：当前用两种颜色激光准备玻璃容器中的气态铷；先前基础无线电/耳机装置用铯，不可互换。
  证据 P5 UTF16 [1272,1428)：The team previously used the setup with cesium atoms to demonstrate the basic radio receiver and a “headphone” appliance to boost sensitivity a hundredfold.
- **信号链**：视频输入调制载波，喇叭天线把传输导向原子，检测后的输出送到电视，模数转换为VGA显示格式。
  证据 P6 UTF16 [1674,1774)：An analog-to-digital converter transforms the signal into a video graphics array format for display.
- **评估基准**：将原始载波作参考，与原子检测的最终视频输出比较来评估系统。
  证据 P7 UTF16 [1979,2129)：Researchers use the original carrier signal as a reference and compare it to the final video output detected through the atoms to evaluate the system.
- **光束因果**：更小光束令原子停留时间更短，刷新率更高、分辨率更好；停留时间与带宽反相关。
  证据 P8 UTF16 [2364,2488)：This time is inversely related to the bandwidth of the receiver; that is, a shorter time and smaller beam produce more data.
- **实验数量与状态**：两束激光直径小于100微米时响应更快且可彩色接收；约100兆比特/秒，带宽和速率仍在研究提升。
  证据 P9 UTF16 [2771,2914)：The system achieved a data rate on the order of 100 megabits per second, considered an excellent speed for video gaming and household internet.

指代与逻辑：

- This time：指原子在激光相互作用区的平均停留时间。（P8）
- This input：回指前句现场视频信号/视频游戏的输入，而非最终输出。（P7）
- it：it 指原始载波信号。（P7）
