The project converts the existing ARPAbet phoneme symbols to broad American
IPA; it does not predict sounds from spelling. Stress digits are inherited;
maximal English onset formatting places them before the following syllable
onset, and monosyllables omit a redundant primary-stress mark. This formatting
is not independently phonetician-calibrated for each word. Broad IPA omits
some allophonic/regional detail. No GPL cmudict-tools implementation or table
is included. CMUdict itself acknowledges possible errors and inconsistencies.

Two CMUdict gaps (alga, destine) use transcriptions from Wiktionary
contributors, available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Preserve attribution, source links,
revision identifiers and the license link in us-supplemental.json:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Alga explicitly carries a shared UK/US label. Destine's source is unlabelled
English; its broad transcription is used as dialect-shared, without claiming
a separately certified American recording. Corresponding adaptations remain
under CC BY-SA 4.0. No Wiktionary remote audio is redistributed.

Project-authored examples and Chinese translations retain per-word actual AI
generation/review provenance. The contextual meaning is the taught sense;
dictionaryMeaning preserves the ECDICT-derived excerpt. Every entry must have
its own non-template sentence, translation, contextual Chinese meaning and
provenance before the release build succeeds. AI/structural checks do not
constitute independent human review, CEFR calibration or an IELTS outcome.

The count is 8000 distinct single dictionary headwords. Source inflections,
names and abbreviations are excluded, and regional spelling variants do not
inflate the count. Derivational words with separate meanings may both occur;
this is not a claim of 8000 calibrated word families or full sense coverage.
The original course words/phrases and all learner records are preserved.

Reconstruct using Node.js (no third-party build dependencies):
  node tools/fetch-essential-vocabulary-source.cjs
  node tools/build-essential-vocabulary.cjs --seed-only --without-pronunciations
  node tools/build-us-pronunciations.cjs
  node tools/build-essential-vocabulary.cjs --examples-from-asset assets/essential-vocabulary-data.js
For a new generation run, replace --examples-from-asset with:
  --examples work/essential-vocabulary/examples-reviewed.jsonl
The published asset contains all reviewed examples and their provenance, so
reconstruction does not require a private API key or ignored generation cache.

audio.lang=en-US prepares an American synthesis/playback request. A request,
an audio filename or a successful MP3 generation does not independently prove
that a synthesis engine precisely followed every IPA phoneme. No engine-to-IPA
alignment or learner pronunciation achievement is certified by this pack.
