# 40篇英语短文 · 英式跟读学习包

先打开「开始学习.html」。全部解压后保持文件夹结构，用电脑浏览器打开；音频可直接拷贝到手机播放器。页面、MP3与讲义均可离线使用，页面不上传答案。

## 你拿到的内容

- 40篇课文：每篇英式正常语速及慢速意群留白 MP3，各1份，共80份。另附40份跟读字幕。
- 每篇3个重点语法解析、6组搭配、2个发音提示；这些是重点精选，不声称涵盖所有词汇或语法。
- 每篇6道搭配回忆、1道语法题、2道理解题、1段听写，以及一个口头/书面输出任务。
- 原文纯英文对照、局部校订记录、复习安排与可下载的学习记录。

## 从多少分钟开始

先用第一篇试学，不必提前承诺固定时长。30分钟基础版：3分钟不看文本听大意；7分钟看文本并跟读；5分钟记6个搭配（来不及先记3个）；5分钟读语法、做语法题；5分钟自测；5分钟脱稿复述或背3个句子。

约60分钟完整版：5分钟复习旧课；5分钟听两遍；15分钟逐句跟读；10分钟词组和语法；10分钟听写与自测；10分钟复述/背诵；5分钟记录错误。若当天想逐字背下整篇，可按需要延长，不以一次流利朗读代替长期记忆。

完成第一篇后记录实际耗时。连续两天感到吃力，就保留一天一篇的接触量，将“全文背诵”改为“先背3–5句重点句，再复述大意”。

## 发音练法

意群列表和当前播放提示区已配有中文翻译，默认显示；点击“隐藏中文翻译”即可只看英文，再点“显示中文翻译”恢复。设置会在本浏览器中保留，切换课文也会沿用。

如果生词多，建议先对照中文弄懂意思，再隐藏中文跟读，最后不看文字听一遍。卡住时只查看不懂的意群，不必强迫自己从一开始就全英文。中文按上下文解释，一条意群可能只是完整句子的一部分，不应逐字机械对应。有关使用母语帮助理解新词，可参考 [Paul Nation：Translanguaging and Vocabulary Learning](https://tesl-ej.org/wordpress/issues/volume26/ej103/ej103a24/)。这里的三步练法是针对本学习包的使用建议。

先听正常版，再听慢速意群留白版。流程是“听一段 → 在空白处跟读 → 下一段”，无需手动暂停。根据从句、并列关系和短语意义分段，保留固定搭配。这是一种便于练习的英语意群划分，不是唯一断句方式。

声音采用英式 -25% 慢速，每个意群后插入与该段朗读等长的空白：读2秒，空2秒；读4秒，空4秒。篇名也有跟读留白，最后一段后也保留跟读时间。播放器请保持1倍速。页面可查看每个意群、当前是“先听”还是“轮到你跟读”，并点击某一意群从该段开始播放。下载后的MP3本身就含有空白，无需依靠页面运行。

用手机录下自己20–30秒的朗读，与同一段音频比较：重音是否相同、词尾是否漏读、停顿是否在意群边界、有无多加元音。每次只改1–2个问题。页面不具备自动发音评分，文字题分数也不能说明口音水平。

## 自测和复习

搭配6分、语法1分，共7分由页面按预设答案核对。输入本课搭配的原形或注明的原文形式；人物代词等其他合理变化可能需要自行对照。理解题2题各1分，按意思核对；听写1分，忽略大小写标点后至少90%的词写对才计分。合计10分，仅用于个人学习，不对应雅思分数。

8分及以上并能在不看原文时复述60秒，可进入下一课；6–7分，次日先补10分钟；5分以下，重做错题和慢速跟读。这个门槛是本包的练习建议。

每篇在学习后第1、3、7天复习：遮住搭配说英文、抽背两句、再次复述。Day N 对应 N+1、N+3、N+7。40篇结束后的复习会延续到Day47。另附可选Day41–60双篇复盘表，明确是复习，不是新增20篇。

输出任务按四项各0–2分自评：内容是否完整、两个搭配是否用对、目标句型是否正确、朗读是否容易听懂。0=未做到，1=部分做到，2=基本做到；共8分，不换算雅思分数。开放题不设唯一答案。

# Day 01 · 横穿加拿大的旅行

A Journey across Canada · PDF标题页 1 · 原编号 未标号

## 学习版正文（与音频一致）

After a quiz last autumn, Kuang crossed the continent eastward to Toronto to visit his schoolmate, the distance measuring approximately 5,000 kilometers. His train started from Vancouver, a city surrounded by mountains. After confirming his baggage was aboard the train, Kuang settled down in his seat. Having a gift for communication, he started chatting with another passenger within 5 minutes. Their topics included the Canadian traditions, the Prime Minister, the mixture of races, and the terrifying Great Fall. After a nice buffet at noon, he was pleased to find that the scenery was impressive. He saw beautiful harbours in the distance, wealthy urban areas and maple forests that covered thousands of acres. He even managed to catch sight of an eagle flying upward over bushes. Kuang reached Toronto which lies near the border at a misty dawn. There was frost and the broad downtown streets were very quiet. Though it was early, Kuang phoned his schoolmate in a booth nearby at once rather than waiting for him to come. They had a good time together.

## 发音提示

- approximately /əˈprɒksɪmətli/：重音在 prox；别把每个音节读得一样重。
- scenery /ˈsiːnəri/：首音节长 /iː/，词尾不是重音。

## 重点搭配

- **settle down**：安顿下来；平静下来
- **have a gift for**：在……方面有天赋
- **chat with**：与……聊天
- **in the distance**：在远处
- **catch sight of**：看见；瞥见
- **rather than**：而不是

## 语法拆解

- **the distance measuring approximately 5,000 kilometers**：独立主格：名词 the distance 有自己的逻辑主语，measuring 描述伴随信息。可改为 and the distance measured approximately 5,000 kilometres。
- **a city surrounded by mountains**：过去分词作后置定语，相当于 a city that is surrounded by mountains；城市是“被群山环绕”的。
- **Having a gift for communication, he started chatting**：现在分词短语作原因状语，having 的逻辑主语是 he；此处 having 不是完成时。

1. 写出英语搭配：安顿下来；平静下来
2. 写出英语搭配：在……方面有天赋
3. 写出英语搭配：与……聊天
4. 写出英语搭配：在远处
5. 写出英语搭配：看见；瞥见
6. 写出英语搭配：而不是
7. 语法填空：a city ____ by mountains (surround)
8. Where did Kuang’s train start?
9. What did he do instead of waiting for his schoolmate?
10. 从音频中听写一段，再与下方目标句核对。初次定位可先看目标句，正式听写请隔至少10分钟并遮住文字。

## 输出任务

用过去时复述一次旅行，至少用 in the distance 和 catch sight of，并加入一个过去分词定语。

## 答案（先作答再看）

1. settle down
2. have a gift for
3. chat with
4. in the distance
5. catch sight of
6. rather than
7. surrounded；city 承受 surround 的动作，过去分词作定语。

9. He phoned his schoolmate from a nearby booth.

## 阅读提示

Canadian traditions 是“加拿大的传统”，不是“加拿大总统”；slightly 是“稍微”，不是“黎明”；acre 是“英亩”。Great Fall 是原文用语，未据此推断具体景点名称。

- over bushes Kuang → over bushes. Kuang
- maple forest → maple forests

# Day 02 · 有趣的节日

An Interesting Festival · PDF标题页 3 · 原编号 10

## 学习版正文（与音频一致）

The Agricultural Feast takes place after Independence Day. It is not a worldwide celebration. Only Christians in Mexico look forward to its arrival for its religious origin. Long ago, humans’ ancestors were bad. They fooled and played tricks on each other and never kept their word. So God turned up and drowned all their crops. Humans starved day and night, weeping. In order to gain God’s forgiveness, a woman poet set off to see God. She admired God and kept apologizing. God was moved. With his permission, humans finally had good harvests again. So, to wipe away sadness and remind themselves of the belief in God, people began the festival. During the festival, people gather in the open air, such as playgrounds or parking lots, and energetically have fun with each other all night long, as though they were never tired. When a beauty dressed up in lovely clothing reads poems in memory of the poet, everyone holds their breath. Then, it is the custom that the bone of a rooster’s head is given to her as an award. Obviously, the story is not true, but the festival is interesting.

## 发音提示

- religious /rɪˈlɪdʒəs/：重音在第二音节，/dʒ/ 不拆开。
- breath /breθ/：末尾是清辅音 /θ/；动词 breathe 的末音不同。

## 重点搭配

- **look forward to**：期待（to 后接名词或动名词）
- **play tricks on**：捉弄
- **keep one’s word**：遵守诺言（原文 kept their word）
- **set off**：出发
- **in memory of**：纪念
- **hold one’s breath**：屏住呼吸（原文 holds their breath）

## 语法拆解

- **look forward to its arrival**：to 是介词；换成动词应写 look forward to arriving，而非 arrive。
- **as though they were never tired**：as though 引导方式状语从句；were 在这里表达与实际不完全相符的假设。
- **a beauty dressed up in lovely clothing**：dressed up 是过去分词短语，修饰前面的名词；可展开为 who is dressed up。

# Day 03 · 均衡饮食

Balanced Diet · PDF标题页 5 · 原编号 15

## 学习版正文（与音频一致）

Wang Peng earned his living by running a barbecue restaurant, which served delicious bacon, fried chicken breast and mutton roasted with pepper and garlic. But his food and discount attracted fewer and fewer customers. Finally, he was in debt. Yong Hui’s slimming restaurant served fresh peas, carrots, eggplants, and raw cucumbers with vinegar. As the hostess, she said fibres benefited customers' digestion the most. In order not to let Yong Hui get away with telling lies, Wang Peng spied on Yong Hui despite her glare. But he was surprised that she was losing her customers, too. Curiosity drove Wang Peng to consult an expert. The expert sighed and said, “Both of your menus have weaknesses and limited strengths. Your customers put on weight too easily, while Yong Hui’s lose weight too quickly. So, Wang Peng, cut down on the fat in your food and increase vegetables and fruits, like nuts, beans, mushrooms, peaches and lemons. You ought to combine the two menus and provide a balanced diet.” Before long, Wang Peng won his customers back.

## 发音提示

- vegetables /ˈvedʒtəblz/：常读成三个音节，避免逐字母读。
- debt /det/：b 不发音。

## 重点搭配

- **earn one’s living**：谋生（原文 earned his living）
- **in debt**：负债
- **get away with**：做坏事而未受惩罚
- **spy on**：暗中监视（原文 spied on）
- **put on weight**：增加体重
- **cut down on**：减少摄入或使用

## 语法拆解

- **by running a barbecue restaurant**：by + 动名词表示手段：通过经营餐馆谋生。

# Day 04 · 一场误会

A Misunderstanding · PDF标题页 7 · 原编号 19

## 学习版正文（与音频一致）

In general, spoken statements are the major way of communication, but body language and facial expressions also have such kinds of function. For example, yawning means not being interested and turning one’s back to someone or swinging your fist shows your anger. But what those gestures really mean is subjective in different cultures. Thus, misunderstandings happen now and then in today’s world of cultural crossroads. Once, representing the Adults’ Education Association, I went to the airport to meet an official of high rank from Colombia and take him to his dormitory and then to the canteen. After the flight arrived, I saw a man looking around curiously. So I approached to greet him. He suddenly dashed to hug me and kissed me on both cheeks. As a young girl, I felt truly embarrassed and put up my hands to defend myself. His false smile told me that he had lost face. Later, I received a cassette from him, in which he recorded his apology. On hearing that Colombians were more likely to kiss others, I soon felt at ease.

## 发音提示

- gesture /ˈdʒestʃə/：首音 /dʒ/，不是 /g/。
- embarrassed /ɪmˈbærəst/：重音在第二音节，末尾 /st/ 不另加元音。

## 重点搭配

- **in general**：一般来说
- **now and then**：时不时
- **look around**：环顾四周（原文 looking around）
- **lose face**：丢脸（原文 lost face）
- **be likely to**：很可能（原文 were more likely to）
