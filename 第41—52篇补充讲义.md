# 第41—52篇 · 新编补充阅读

本册是新编学习材料，不是原PDF缺失页或雅思真题。全部人物、研究情节及图表数字为教学设定。每篇四段，每段可单独跟读。

## 第41篇 · A Course That Opened a Door

一门打开机会之门的课程

### 第1段

When Mei returned to college, she needed a course that would fit around her job. The college offered tuition in the evening, but enrolment was falling. Some applicants lacked childcare, while others could not afford the fees. A tutor asked Mei to help investigate the problem.

梅重返学院时，需要一门能配合工作时间的课程。学院提供晚间教学，但报名人数正在下降。有些申请者缺少托育支持，另一些人则付不起学费。一位导师请梅帮忙调查原因。

### 第2段

Mei interviewed several adults about their access to education. Each learner had a different timetable, and much of the advice online was confusing. There was little information about financial support. She realised that a flexible curriculum would be useful only if people could actually attend the lessons.

梅采访了几位成年人，了解他们接受教育的机会。每位学习者的时间安排不同，网上的许多建议又令人困惑。有关经济支持的信息很少。她意识到，灵活的课程只有在人们确实能来上课时才有用。

### 第3段

The college has introduced a small grant for eligible applicants. Since September, Mei has been helping new students complete their forms. She completed her own application last week. The new assessment includes a practical project, so students can demonstrate skills as well as remember facts.

学院已经为符合条件的申请者推出了一项小额助学金。自九月以来，梅一直在帮助新生填写表格。她上周完成了自己的申请。新的考核包含一个实践项目，让学生既能展示技能，也能记忆知识。

### 第4段

Mei still finds some assignments difficult, but she no longer studies alone. Her group meets every Thursday to exchange ideas and give feedback. A certificate will not solve every problem; however, the experience has given her the confidence to apply for a better job.

梅仍觉得有些作业很难，但她不再独自学习。她的小组每周四见面，交流想法并提供反馈。一张证书不会解决所有问题，不过，这段经历让她有信心申请更好的工作。

### 重点词汇

- **tuition /tjuˈɪʃən/**：n. 教学；指导。搭配：tuition fees 是学费；tuition 本身常指教学。
- **enrolment /ɪnˈrəʊlmənt/**：n. 注册；入学人数。enrol + -ment；这里采用英式拼写。
- **childcare /ˈtʃaɪldkeə/**：n. 儿童照护。child + care，合成词。
- **curriculum /kəˈrɪkjələm/**：n. 课程体系。词族：curricular；常见复数 curricula，也可用 curriculums。
- **grant /ɡrɑːnt/**：n. 补助金。搭配：a grant for students。
- **eligible /ˈelɪdʒəbəl/**：adj. 符合条件的。搭配：be eligible for；词族 eligibility。
- **applicants /ˈæplɪkənts/**：n. 申请者（复数）。apply → applicant；-ant 可表示人，复数加 -s。
- **assignments /əˈsaɪnmənts/**：n. 作业；任务（复数）。assign + -ment → assignment，再加 -s。
- **feedback /ˈfiːdbæk/**：n. 反馈。通常不可数：some feedback，不写 a feedback。
- **certificate /səˈtɪfɪkət/**：n. 证书。词族：certify → certificate；这里名词末尾读 /kət/。

### 语法

The college has introduced a small grant

现在完成时与一般过去时：has introduced 强调现有成果；completed ... last week 指明已经结束的过去时间。

Mei has been helping new students

现在完成进行时：has been doing 强调从过去延续至今的活动，since September 给出起点。

Each learner had a different timetable

数量词与不可数名词：each + 单数名词；much advice、little information。advice 和 information 通常不加 -s。

### 固定搭配

- access to education：接受教育的机会
- financial support：经济支持
- complete an application：完成申请
- as well as：以及；除……之外还
- give feedback：提供反馈
- apply for a job：申请工作

### 自测与参考答案

Since September, Mei has ____ helping new students. (be)

答案：been。has been doing 构成现在完成进行时。

Why could some adults not attend college?

参考：They lacked childcare or could not afford the fees.

How does the new assessment allow students to show their skills?

参考：It includes a practical project.

输出任务：介绍你正在学习的一项技能：用现在完成时写一个成果，用现在完成进行时写一项持续努力，再说明你需要什么支持。

## 第42篇 · Finding a Home Near Work

寻找离工作近的住处

### 第1段

Daniel accepted a job in a growing city and began looking for a flat. An advertisement described one property as affordable, but the rent was higher than he expected. The landlord also requested a deposit. Daniel calculated his monthly expenditure before arranging a viewing.

丹尼尔在一座发展中的城市找到工作，开始寻找公寓。广告称一处房源价格实惠，但租金比他预想的高。房东还要求支付押金。丹尼尔先算了每月开支，再安排看房。

### 第2段

The first flat was close to the office, whereas the second was near a railway station. The nearer he lived to work, the less he would spend on transport. However, the cheaper flat had more space. He realised that affordability depended on the whole budget, not simply the rent.

第一套公寓靠近办公室，第二套则靠近火车站。他住得离工作地点越近，交通花费就越少。不过，较便宜的公寓空间更大。他意识到，是否负担得起取决于整个预算，不能只看租金。

### 第3段

A housing adviser explained that a tenant should read the agreement carefully. She asked whether repairs were included and who was responsible for maintenance. Daniel made a list of questions instead of signing immediately. There were fewer vacant flats than he had expected, but he refused to rush.

一位住房顾问解释，租客应仔细阅读协议。她询问租金是否包含维修，以及谁负责维护。丹尼尔列出了问题清单，没有马上签字。空置公寓比他预想的少，但他没有仓促决定。

### 第4段

Eventually, he chose to share a flat with a colleague. Their commute was longer, yet their combined bills were manageable. The neighbourhood had a library and a small park. Daniel could now save a little each month without giving up every activity he enjoyed.

最后，他选择与一位同事合租。通勤时间更长了，但合计的账单仍在承受范围内。社区有图书馆和小公园。丹尼尔现在每月能存下一点钱，不用放弃自己喜欢的所有活动。

### 重点词汇

- **affordable /əˈfɔːdəbəl/**：adj. 负担得起的。afford + -able；名词 affordability。
- **landlord /ˈlændlɔːd/**：n. 房东。整体记忆：tenant 是租客，与 landlord 对照。
- **deposit /dɪˈpɒzɪt/**：n. 押金。搭配：pay a deposit。
- **expenditure /ɪkˈspendɪtʃə/**：n. 开支。词族：expend；与 income 收入对照。
- **tenant /ˈtenənt/**：n. 租客。搭配：a tenant in a flat。
- **maintenance /ˈmeɪntənəns/**：n. 维护；保养。maintain → maintenance，留意拼写变化。
- **vacant /ˈveɪkənt/**：adj. 空置的。词族：vacancy；a vacant flat。
- **commute /kəˈmjuːt/**：n. 通勤路程。也可作动词 commute to work。
- **neighbourhood /ˈneɪbəhʊd/**：n. 街区；社区。neighbour + -hood；英式拼写含 ou。
- **affordability /əˌfɔːdəˈbɪləti/**：n. 可负担性。affordable → affordability；-able 与 -ability 对照。

### 语法

The nearer he lived to work, the less he would spend

关联比较级：the + 比较级，the + 比较级，表示“越……越……”。

There were fewer vacant flats

fewer 与 less：fewer 修饰可数名词复数 flats；less 修饰不可数的 money、space 等。

Daniel accepted a job

冠词：a job 首次提到一份工作；the rent、the first flat 指语境中明确的租金或房子。复数或不可数名词泛指时常用零冠词，如 repairs、maintenance。

### 固定搭配

- pay a deposit：支付押金
- monthly expenditure：每月开支
- be responsible for：负责
- read the agreement：阅读协议
- share a flat：合租公寓
- give up：放弃

### 自测与参考答案

There were ____ vacant flats than he expected. (fewer / less)

答案：fewer。flats 是可数名词复数，用 fewer。

Why did Daniel calculate more than the rent?

参考：He needed to consider his whole budget, including transport and bills.

What did he eventually decide to do?

参考：He decided to share a flat with a colleague.

输出任务：比较两处住处，用 whereas 写一个对比，再用 the more / less ... the more / less ... 写一句权衡。

## 第43篇 · A Different Working Week

不一样的工作周

### 第1段

Leah manages a small design team. During a busy month, several employees asked for more autonomy. They wanted to choose where they worked on two days each week. Leah was interested in the proposal, but she worried about communication and the quality of customer service.

利娅管理一个小型设计团队。在一个忙碌的月份，几位员工希望获得更多自主权。他们想每周有两天自己选择工作地点。利娅对提议感兴趣，但担心沟通和客户服务质量。

### 第2段

If the team works remotely, it will need a clear schedule. Leah therefore arranged a trial rather than making a permanent promise. If everyone worked different hours, meetings would be difficult to organise. She asked each employee to explain which tasks required collaboration and which could be done independently.

如果团队远程工作，就需要明确的时间安排。因此，利娅安排了一次试行，而没有作出永久承诺。如果每个人的工作时间都不同，会议就会很难安排。她请每位员工说明哪些任务需要合作，哪些可以独立完成。

### 第3段

After six weeks, productivity had improved on some tasks, but the workload was not shared equally. One colleague felt isolated, while another enjoyed avoiding the daily journey. Their supervisor introduced a short morning meeting and a weekly mentoring session. The team began to judge progress by results rather than time online.

六周后，有些任务的生产效率提高了，但工作量分配不均。一位同事觉得孤单，另一位则很高兴不用每天通勤。主管安排了简短晨会和每周一次的指导交流。团队开始根据结果，而不是在线时长，判断进展。

### 第4段

Leah concluded that flexibility needed boundaries. Employees had to remain available during agreed hours, although they could plan the rest of the day themselves. Higher job satisfaction was a welcome outcome, but no single arrangement suited everyone. The team agreed to review the trial before extending it.

利娅认为，灵活安排需要有边界。员工必须在约定时间内保持可联系状态，但可以自己安排一天中的其他时间。更高的工作满意度是可喜的结果，但没有一种安排适合所有人。团队同意先评估，再决定是否延长试行。

### 重点词汇

- **autonomy /ɔːˈtɒnəmi/**：n. 自主权。auto- 与“自己”有关；词族 autonomous。
- **remotely /rɪˈməʊtli/**：adv. 远程地。remote + -ly，保留 e。
- **collaboration /kəˌlæbəˈreɪʃən/**：n. 协作。collaborate → collaboration；去 e 加 -ion。
- **productivity /ˌprɒdʌkˈtɪvəti/**：n. 生产效率。productive → productivity；与 production 区分。
- **workload /ˈwɜːkləʊd/**：n. 工作量。work + load。
- **supervisor /ˈsuːpəvaɪzə/**：n. 主管。supervise → supervisor；去 e 加 -or。
- **mentoring /ˈmentɔːrɪŋ/**：n. 指导；带教。mentor 作动词加 -ing；这里是名词性用法。
- **flexibility /ˌfleksəˈbɪləti/**：n. 灵活性。flexible → flexibility，-ible 与 -ibility 对照。
- **satisfaction /ˌsætɪsˈfækʃən/**：n. 满意。satisfy → satisfaction；job satisfaction。
- **boundaries /ˈbaʊndəriz/**：n. 边界；界限（复数）。boundary 的 y 变 i，加 -es。

### 语法

If the team works remotely, it will need a clear schedule

第一条件句：if + 一般现在时，主句用 will + 原形，讨论现实中的未来可能性。

If everyone worked different hours, meetings would be difficult

第二条件句：if + 过去式，主句 would + 原形，讨论假设安排；过去式不一定指过去时间。

although they could plan the rest of the day themselves

让步从句：although 后接完整句子，主句通常不再加 but。

### 固定搭配

- work remotely：远程工作
- arrange a trial：安排试行
- share the workload：分担工作量
- judge progress by results：根据结果判断进展
- job satisfaction：工作满意度
- remain available：保持可联系状态

### 自测与参考答案

If everyone worked different hours, meetings ____ be difficult. (will / would)

答案：would。这里是假设安排，用第二条件句 would + 原形。

Why did Leah start with a trial?

参考：She wanted to explore the proposal while checking communication and service quality.

What problem appeared during the trial?

参考：The workload was not shared equally and one colleague felt isolated.

输出任务：谈谈你理想的工作或学习安排，用一个现实条件句和一个假设条件句，说明优点与可能的问题。

## 第44篇 · Before Clicking Agree

点击同意之前

### 第1段

Omar downloaded an app that promised to organise his study notes. Before he could use it, a message requested consent to collect personal data. The document was long, and he nearly accepted it without reading. Then he noticed that the app wanted access to his location even when it was closed.

奥马尔下载了一款声称能整理学习笔记的应用。使用前，一条消息请求他同意收集个人数据。文件很长，他差点没读就接受了。随后，他发现应用甚至在关闭时也想获取他的位置信息。

### 第2段

Omar asked his sister, who studies computing, whether this permission was necessary. She explained that an algorithm could use several small details to build a profile. She did not claim that every app was dangerous. Instead, she encouraged him to check the purpose of each request and the company's privacy policy.

奥马尔问学习计算机的姐姐，这项权限是否必要。姐姐解释，算法可以利用多个小细节建立用户画像。她没有说所有应用都危险，而是建议他检查每项请求的目的和公司的隐私政策。

### 第3段

The company said that it stored information securely. Omar wanted to know how long the records would be retained and whether users could delete them. Strong encryption might reduce some risks, but it would not explain why unnecessary information was collected. A security claim needed evidence, not just an attractive label.

公司称其安全存储信息。奥马尔想知道记录会保留多久，以及用户能否删除它们。强加密或许能降低某些风险，却不能解释为什么要收集不必要的信息。安全声明需要证据，而不仅是吸引人的标签。

### 第4段

Omar eventually chose a version with fewer permissions. He also disabled notifications during study time. The decision did not make him an expert, but it gave him more control. He learned to ask a practical question: does the convenience justify the information I am being asked to share?

奥马尔最终选择了权限更少的版本。他也在学习时关闭通知。这个决定没有让他变成专家，但让他获得了更多控制权。他学会了问一个实际问题：这份便利，值得我分享所要求的信息吗？

### 重点词汇

- **consent /kənˈsent/**：n. 同意；许可。搭配：give consent；此处通常不可数。
- **algorithm /ˈælɡərɪðəm/**：n. 算法。整体记忆，注意结尾 /ðəm/。
- **profile /ˈprəʊfaɪl/**：n. 用户画像；档案。搭配：build a profile。
- **privacy /ˈprɪvəsi/**：n. 隐私。常见英式读音 /ˈprɪvəsi/；privacy policy。
- **securely /sɪˈkjʊəli/**：adv. 安全地。secure + -ly；保留 e。
- **retained /rɪˈteɪnd/**：v. 被保留（分词形式）。retain + -ed；原句 would be retained 是被动结构。
- **encryption /ɪnˈkrɪpʃən/**：n. 加密。encrypt → encryption，词尾拼写发生变化。
- **permissions /pəˈmɪʃənz/**：n. 权限（复数）。一般“许可”常不可数；计算机中的一项项权限可用复数。
- **notifications /ˌnəʊtɪfɪˈkeɪʃənz/**：n. 通知（复数）。notify → notification，再加 -s。
- **justify /ˈdʒʌstɪfaɪ/**：v. 证明……合理。词族：justification；justify a decision。

### 语法

The company said that it stored information securely

间接引语：主句 said 为过去时，从句可将现在时回移为过去时；若强调至今仍成立，也可能保留现在时。

how long the records would be retained

间接问句与被动：how long + 主语 + would be + 过去分词；不写 how long would the records ...。

Strong encryption might reduce some risks

情态动词与谨慎表达：might + 动词原形表示可能性，避免把未经证实的推测写成确定事实。

### 固定搭配

- personal data：个人数据
- give consent：给予同意
- privacy policy：隐私政策
- reduce risks：降低风险
- disable notifications：关闭通知
- justify a decision：证明决定合理

### 自测与参考答案

The records would be ____ for one month. (retain)

答案：retained。would be + 过去分词表示被动。

Why did Omar question the location request?

参考：The app wanted his location even when it was closed.

Why was encryption not a complete answer to his concern?

参考：It did not explain why unnecessary information was collected.

输出任务：介绍一个应用的便利和隐私顾虑，用 might 或 may 表达可能性，再写一个含 whether 的间接问句。

## 第45篇 · A Town Plans Its Water Supply

小镇规划供水

### 第1段

After a dry summer, a small town reviewed its water supply. The reservoir was lower than usual, and demand had increased as new homes were built. At a public meeting, an engineer explained that the town needed a plan for both ordinary use and a possible drought.

一个干旱夏季之后，一座小镇重新审视供水情况。水库水位比平时低，而新房建设带来了需求增长。在公开会议上，一位工程师解释，小镇需要同时考虑日常用水和可能发生的旱情。

### 第2段

Water is collected, treated and distributed through a network of pipes. Some of those pipes had been installed many years earlier. A survey found leakage in several streets. Repairing the network would reduce waste, although the council still needed to compare the cost with other options.

水被收集、处理，再通过管道网络输送出去。其中一些管道早在多年前就已安装。调查发现几条街道存在漏水。维修管网可以减少浪费，但地方议会仍需将成本与其他方案比较。

### 第3段

The engineer distinguished water conservation from water treatment. Filtration removes certain materials, while other stages deal with different forms of contamination. A proposed upgrade would be assessed before construction began. No one was asked to copy the treatment process at home; the discussion concerned the town's public infrastructure.

工程师区分了节水与水处理。过滤去除某些物质，其他环节处理不同类型的污染。拟议的升级工程会在开工前接受评估。没有人被要求在家模仿处理流程；讨论关注的是小镇的公共基础设施。

### 第4段

The council also invited farmers to discuss irrigation and seasonal demand. If rainfall remained low, restrictions might become necessary. Residents wanted a reliable supply without a large increase in charges. The final proposal combined pipe repairs, clearer information and a review of long-term capacity, rather than relying on a single solution.

地方议会也邀请农民讨论灌溉和季节性需求。如果降雨持续偏少，可能就需要限水。居民既希望供水可靠，也不希望收费大幅上涨。最终方案结合管道维修、更清晰的信息和长期供水能力评估，没有只依靠一种措施。

### 重点词汇

- **reservoir /ˈrezəvwɑː/**：n. 水库。搭配：a water reservoir；词尾不按字母逐个读。
- **drought /draʊt/**：n. 干旱。与 doubt /daʊt/ 对比，注意 /r/。
- **distributed /dɪˈstrɪbjuːtɪd/**：v. 被输送；被分配（分词形式）。distribute + -d；are/is distributed 表被动。
- **leakage /ˈliːkɪdʒ/**：n. 渗漏。leak + -age。
- **filtration /fɪlˈtreɪʃən/**：n. 过滤。词族：filter → filtration，不能直接机械拼接。
- **contamination /kənˌtæmɪˈneɪʃən/**：n. 污染。contaminate 去 e 加 -ion。
- **infrastructure /ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃə/**：n. 基础设施。infra- 有“下面”之意；整体指公共基础设施。
- **irrigation /ˌɪrɪˈɡeɪʃən/**：n. 灌溉。irrigate 去 e 加 -ion。
- **restrictions /rɪˈstrɪkʃənz/**：n. 限制措施（复数）。restrict + -ion → restriction，再加 -s。
- **capacity /kəˈpæsəti/**：n. 容量；能力。搭配：water supply capacity。

### 语法

Water is collected, treated and distributed

流程被动语态：is + 三个并列过去分词；描述一般流程使用一般现在时。

Some of those pipes had been installed

过去完成时被动：had been + 过去分词，安装发生在过去的会议或调查之前。

Repairing the network would reduce waste

动名词作主语：Repairing the network 作为整体；would 在过去叙事中表示预期结果。

### 固定搭配

- water supply：供水
- seasonal demand：季节性需求
- reduce waste：减少浪费
- public infrastructure：公共基础设施
- compare the cost with：将成本与……比较
- rely on：依靠

## 第51篇 · Testing a New Packaging Idea

检验新的包装方案

### 第1段

In a college workshop, Amir developed a container intended to replace some disposable packaging. His first prototype looked promising, but appearance was not enough. He needed to find out whether the material could protect its contents during an ordinary delivery and whether customers could reuse it easily.

在学院工作室里，阿米尔开发了一种容器，希望替代部分一次性包装。第一个原型看起来不错，但外观还不够。他需要弄清材料能否在普通配送中保护内装物，以及顾客是否容易重复使用它。

### 第2段

Amir designed an experiment with help from a tutor. The material was the main variable, while the size of each container remained constant. He recorded a hypothesis before beginning the trial. This helped him avoid changing the question simply because he preferred one result.

阿米尔在导师帮助下设计了实验。材料是主要变量，每个容器的尺寸则保持不变。他在试验开始前记录了一项假设，这有助于避免因为偏爱某个结果，就临时改变研究问题。

### 第3段

After several tests, one design performed better than the others. However, the sample was small, and durability under different conditions remained uncertain. Had he tested only the strongest container, his conclusion would have been misleading. He repeated the procedure and kept a record of unsuccessful attempts as well as successful ones.

几轮测试后，一个设计表现优于其他设计。不过，样本较小，不同条件下的耐用性仍不确定。如果他只测试最坚固的容器，结论就会具有误导性。他重复了流程，也保留了失败和成功尝试的记录。

### 第4段

The team then considered the environmental impact across the product's lifecycle. A reusable container still required resources to manufacture and transport. Amir decided that feasibility involved cost, convenience and likely reuse, not merely a successful demonstration. He presented the limitations openly and suggested further research before making a broad commercial claim.

团队随后考虑产品整个生命周期的环境影响。可重复使用的容器仍需资源来制造和运输。阿米尔认为，可行性包括成本、便利程度和实际重复使用的可能性，不只是一次成功展示。他公开说明局限，并建议在作出广泛商业宣传前进一步研究。

- **prototype /ˈprəʊtətaɪp/**：n. 原型。proto- 表最初；prototype testing。

- **procedure /prəˈsiːdʒə/**：n. 程序；步骤。搭配：repeat the procedure。

## 第52篇 · A Budget for Every Generation

兼顾各代人的预算

### 第1段

A town council had enough money to fund only some of its proposed projects. One group wanted a larger playground, while another requested a day centre for older residents. Maya, a volunteer on the committee, believed that the discussion should begin with needs rather than competition between generations.

一座小镇的议会只有足够资金支持部分拟议项目。一组人想要更大的游乐场，另一组人希望为老年居民建日间活动中心。委员会志愿者玛雅认为，讨论应从需求出发，而不是变成代际竞争。

### 第2段

The town's ageing population was increasing demand for accessible services. Meanwhile, families needed affordable childcare and safe places to meet. The number of requests was rising, but the amount of funding was limited. Neither proposal could be judged fairly without information about who would use it and how often.

小镇人口老龄化，使方便老年人使用的服务需求增加。同时，家庭需要可负担的托育和安全聚会场所。申请数量上升，但资金有限。若不了解使用者是谁、使用频率如何，就无法公平评价任何一项提议。

### 第3段

Maya suggested a shared community building. It could host activities for children in the afternoon and social sessions for older people in the morning. This might reduce loneliness and encourage inclusion, although the committee would still need to examine transport, staffing and access. A building alone could not guarantee participation.

玛雅建议共用一栋社区建筑。下午可举办儿童活动，上午可安排老年社交活动。这或许能减轻孤独、促进包容，但委员会仍需考察交通、人员配备和出入条件。一栋建筑本身不能保证人们参与。

### 第4段

The final budget reserved money for a small pilot and an independent evaluation. The committee published its priorities and explained the opportunity cost: funding one project meant delaying another. Maya supported the decision because it included a review. She hoped that transparent planning would help residents discuss public spending with greater trust.

最终预算为小规模试点和独立评估预留了资金。委员会公开了优先事项，并解释机会成本：资助一个项目，就意味着推迟另一个。玛雅支持这个决定，因为其中包含后续评估。她希望透明规划能让居民以更强的信任讨论公共支出。

### 重点词汇

- **ageing /ˈeɪdʒɪŋ/**：adj. 老龄化的。age → ageing，常见英式拼写；此处修饰 population。
- **funding /ˈfʌndɪŋ/**：n. 资金；资助。fund + -ing，通常不可数。
- **loneliness /ˈləʊnlinəs/**：n. 孤独。lonely 的 y 变 i，加 -ness。
- **inclusion /ɪnˈkluːʒən/**：n. 包容；纳入。include → inclusion；social inclusion。
- **staffing /ˈstɑːfɪŋ/**：n. 人员配备。staff 作动词，加 -ing。
- **evaluation /ɪˌvæljuˈeɪʃən/**：n. 评估。evaluate 去 e 加 -ion。
- **priorities /praɪˈɒrətiz/**：n. 优先事项（复数）。priority 的 y 变 i，加 -es。
- **opportunity /ˌɒpəˈtjuːnəti/**：n. 机会。opportunity cost 是“机会成本”，指放弃的替代选择的价值。
- **transparent /trænsˈpærənt/**：adj. 透明的；公开的。词族 transparency；此处指决策过程公开。
- **pilot /ˈpaɪlət/**：n. 试点项目。本课不是“飞行员”；a pilot project。
